## Change-request procedure

For **each** finding marked "Conflict with §2: yes", open **one** GitHub issue (US-151 AC2):

1. **Title:** `Change request: <short description of the conflict>`.
2. **Label:** `change-request`.
3. **Body:**
   - the finding as written in the summary;
   - the interview numbers (never a name or contact detail);
   - the exact FR IDs from §2.2 it conflicts with, and what the requirement says now;
   - the change the finding suggests.
4. **Link back:** put the issue link in the "Change-request issue" column of the summary table.

A change request proposes a change and does not make one. The §2 register, the task register and the decision register change only through a decision recorded by the team (CLAUDE.md §13).
